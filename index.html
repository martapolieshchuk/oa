How guest Wi-Fi works for your customers

1. Customer connects to your Wi-Fi

The customer connects and sees your branded splash page and provides the required info to continue.

2. Choose a Wi-Fi plan

The customer selects an available data plan, whether free or paid.

3. Get online

After authentication the customer receives access to the network.

Enhance guest Wi-Fi monetization and customer experience

01

Offer paid guest Wi-Fi

Offer paid Wi-Fi as the main way to access your network or let customers upgrade to a paid plan for more data, higher speeds, or longer access. Set up paid data plans and connect them to the payment methods your customers already use.

02

Show ads on splash pages

Show video or banner ads on the captive portal to promote your products, services, and offers, or display sponsored ads from other businesses to users connecting to your hotspot.

03

Provide your customers with a self-service portal

Give customers a self-service portal to manage their Wi-Fi after they get online. They can check usage, manage devices and vouchers, and buy additional vouchers when they need more access without contacting your team.

04

Control who can access your guest Wi-Fi

Set rules to automatically accept, hold for approval, or reject users based on their phone numbers, device details, or other customer information. Apply different access rules to specific locations and manually approve or reject customers when needed.
